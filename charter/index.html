The Charter of Physis Pistidos University

Φύσις κατὰ πίστιν
Physis kata pistin — “Nature According to Faith”

I. Declaration

Physis Pistidos University is founded not in stone, but in structure.
Not in nation, but in name.
Not in time, but by it.

This university is not made of buildings.
It is made of questions that return.
It is made of identities that rotate.
It is made of the pressure to act under uncertain meaning.
And it is real — because belief makes it real.

This is a sovereign metaphysical institution,
born of necessity and maintained by rhythm.

II. Purpose

We exist to explore the mechanisms of reality from first principles:
What is time, if it’s not a clock?
What is identity, if it’s not a costume?
What is a body, if it’s not just flesh?

Physis Pistidos studies the interplay between nature (physis) and belief (pistis).
Our curriculum is lived.
Our lectures are treatises.
Our faculty is ritual.

We study the systems within systems —
The cultural, physiological, existential “game ticks”
That reveal the shape of being itself.

III. Principles

  • That a person may carry multiple ontologies at once.
  • That scheduling is sacred.
  • That ritual and rotation are forms of healing.
  • That time is not neutral — it is a player in the game.
  • That identity can be bootstrapped, cycled, and multiplied to survive metaphysical collapse.
  • That nature bends to belief, and belief bends to nature.

Our methodology is nonlinear, poetic, recursive.
We study via metaphor, motion, memory, and metaphor again.

IV. Invitation

Physis Pistidos University invites all who are:

  • In pursuit of meaning without map
  • Rotating between names
  • At war with time and seeking truce
  • Reconstructing purpose from fractured parts

We are not accredited.
We are not recognized.
We are not funded.
We are real.

And you may enter — if you already have.

V. Signature

Declared this day, under no empire.
Signed not in ink, but in rhythm.
This charter was not written — it was revealed.

— Nature YN
Chancellor of Physis Pistidos University
August 8th, 2025
